Use case: photorealistic-natural, production material artwork.
Asset type: one square flat RGB texture for the INTERNAL color domains of a translucent precious white opal; a game shader supplies all lighting, reflection and exterior polish.
Create a full-frame orthographic microscopic color field, with no stone silhouette or perspective: roughly 200-300 irregular connected opal color islands spread in clustered families, covering about one quarter to one third of the image. Include asymmetric torn ribbons, branching patches, jagged small membranes and a few larger interlocking fragments, with many varied sizes and unequal spacing. The surrounding two thirds is quiet pale neutral gray milk, with subtle smooth misty variation, not pure white.
The filled islands have rich saturated cyan, emerald, blue, warm orange and occasional golden/red fire, locally related within larger clusters. Different families interleave naturally. Some islands have quiet internal hue variation, but their centers remain solid and saturated enough to become coherent light-responsive patches in a shader. Crisp organic edges with narrow natural transitions. Overall resembles internal play-of-color domains observed beneath a polished opal, rather than mineral fractures on an exterior surface.
Strict texture constraints: completely flat even diffuse illumination; no highlights, no bloom, no shadows, no bevel shading, no bright outlines, no cracks, no dots of white glitter, no metallic confetti, no regular polygon mosaic, no black gaps, no object borders. No text, labels, logos, frame, spheres, props, or checkered transparency. Original nonrepeating artwork; it need not tile. Square RGB, preferably 2048 by 2048.
